Frequently Asked Questions

General

What does my ticket include?

The ticket includes entry to the movie screening only, unless the event page says otherwise — Gourmet and F&B tickets list exactly what is included.

Will there be food and beverages available at the venue?

Yes. Every screening has food and drinks available for purchase at the venue. Outside food is not allowed.

What is my ticket?

Your ticket is the e-ticket we email and WhatsApp you after booking. Show the QR code at the entrance.

What is the location of the venue?

The full venue name and address are on each event page, and in your booking confirmation.

Are there allocated seats?

Seating is pre-arranged. If you've booked a ticket, you are assured a seat.

Are children allowed?

We follow the film's certificate. U and UA screenings are open to everyone (under-12s with an adult); A-rated films are 18+.

Are there toilet facilities available?

Yes, all our venues have restrooms available for guests.

Can I transfer my tickets to another show?

You can move your booking to another screening in the same city up to 48 hours before showtime. Write to us with your booking ID.

If the show is cancelled will I receive a refund for my payment?

Yes. If we cancel a screening you can move to another date or take a full refund.

What if it rains?

If a screening is called off because of weather, we'll let you know by 4 PM on the day and you can move to another date or take a full refund.

Membership

What does the membership entail?

For ₹2,500 a year you get unlimited access to SCC open-air and Fan Arena screenings, a free +1 at each one, and invites to members-only screenings. Join the club.

As a member how can I book?

Pick a screening on the website and reserve your spot for free with your member ID. At the venue, show any valid government ID.

Can I book more than 1 ticket?

Members can book for themselves plus one guest per Open Air and Fan Arena screening.

Can I book for 2 consecutive shows?

Yes, as long as seats are available at both screenings.

Payment Related

Can I cancel my ticket?

Tickets are non-refundable, but you can transfer them to a friend or move them to another screening up to 48 hours before showtime.

Payment has been deducted but I have not received a ticket or confirmation?

Write to hello@sunsetcinemaclub.in with your payment reference — failed bookings are usually refunded automatically within 5–7 working days.

Miscellaneous

Do you give large group discounts on group bookings?

Yes — for groups of 10 or more, write to us and we will share a group rate.

Can I hire SCC for a private event?

Absolutely — birthdays, proposals, offsites, product launches. Request a private screening.
